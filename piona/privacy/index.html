Privacy Policy

Piona keeps what you create on your device. This page explains exactly what leaves it and what stays.

Last updated 30 September 2026

The short version

Piona does not collect or sell personal data. It has no accounts and no sign-in, no server of its own, and no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting code.

  • Data collected: none.
  • Data linked to you: none.
  • Data used for tracking: none.
  • Third-party software development kits that collect data: none.

What leaves your device

These are Piona's direct content requests. Age-appropriate experience checks run through your platform's service.

Request Host What is sent
Directory search itunes.apple.com Your search term and your device region
Directory lookup itunes.apple.com A show identifier and your device region
Feeds, artwork and audio Wherever each show publishes them Nothing beyond the request itself
Developer photo github.com Nothing beyond the request itself
  • No request includes a device, advertising or account identifier, or any other identifier made up by the app.
  • Piona shares no data with any third party and has no analytics or advertising partners. Each request goes straight from your device to the host listed.
  • Feed, artwork and audio hosts are chosen by the shows you open and follow, not by Piona. Artwork in search results comes from Apple.

What podcast hosts can see

Fetching a feed or an audio file is an ordinary web request to the host that publishes it. As with any app or browser, that host can see your IP address and a user agent. Piona adds no identifier of its own and has no relationship with these hosts.

Some hosts still serve feeds and audio over plain HTTP rather than HTTPS. Piona allows this, because refusing would quietly break those shows. Directory requests and the developer photo always use HTTPS.

What is stored, and where

Everything Piona keeps is stored in the app's private storage on your device:

  • The shows you follow, your playlists and their order, your listening progress and history, and cached feed content.
  • The episode you were last playing, so the player can pick up where you left off.
  • Downloaded audio files.
  • Cached artwork.
  • Permission to read any folder you linked, so that access survives a restart.
  • Your settings: whether explicit content is on, whether finished episodes leave a playlist automatically, whether finished downloads are deleted, and for each folder, which file date to use and whether its episodes can be downloaded.

None of it is synced or sent to Piona. It stays until you remove it or delete the app, and other apps cannot read it.

Folders of your own audio

When you link a folder, Piona reads the names, durations and dates of the audio files in it to build a show, and keeps permission to read the folder again after a restart. Piona does not change your linked folder or upload its files. It may keep a copy of cover art in the app's private on-device cache.

Explicit content and age

Explicit shows and their episodes stay hidden while the setting is off. Where available, Piona asks your platform for an age-appropriate experience result even where law does not require an age check. When Apple's Declared Age Range or Google Play's Age Signals confirms you are 18 or over, Settings shows the explicit-content switch and you choose whether to turn it on or off. If the platform cannot confirm adulthood or the feature is unavailable, access stays restricted.

Your platform may provide Piona an age range or an age-check result. Piona does not ask you to enter your age or date of birth, and does not store your age range. On iOS, Piona saves your last sharing decision and whether the platform confirmed you as 18 or over in private storage on this device. On Android, the result is used only in memory during the current app run. Your explicit-content preference is stored on both platforms. This age information and these decisions are never sent to the developer.

On iOS, Piona requests your age range only after you tap Continue on the Age Confirmation page or choose Age Confirmation in Settings or Library. Its saved decision reflects the last response it received; changes made in system settings are not checked until you request confirmation again. Apple or Google manages age sharing under its own settings and policies, and determines whether a later request shows another prompt.

Children

Piona is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included. The podcasts it plays come from third parties and are not reviewed by Piona. It relies on each publisher's explicit label, which covers a whole show rather than single episodes.

Backups

On Android, Piona turns off automatic backup, so your library is not copied to Google's servers. On iOS, whether Piona's data is included in iCloud or computer backups depends on your device's backup settings. Piona itself uploads nothing.

Testing through TestFlight

While Piona is in testing, it is distributed through TestFlight, which is Apple's service and covered by Apple's privacy policy rather than this one. TestFlight shows the developer each tester's device, system version and app version, how many sessions and crashes there have been, and any feedback you choose to send, which can include a screenshot and crash details. If you join through a public link, your name and email address are not shown; if you were invited by email, they are.

Emailing the developer

In testing builds, How to Report a Problem in Settings ends with a link to email the developer. Tapping it opens your own email app with the address filled in. Piona adds nothing to the message and does not send it; your email app sends it only if you choose to.

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and whatever you write or attach, such as screenshots. It is used only to reply to you and to fix what you reported, and is never used for marketing or passed on to anyone else. To have it deleted, ask at the same address.

Deleting your data

Deleting Piona removes its local app storage. Existing device backups may retain copies according to your platform's backup settings. There is no account to close.

You can also remove parts of it without deleting the app: clear or edit your listening history from Recently Played on Home, remove downloads in Settings, unfollow a show from its page, or delete a playlist.

You can turn explicit content back off in Settings at any time. Deleting the app also withdraws every permission Piona holds, including access to your folders.

Changes to this policy

If the way Piona handles data changes, this page will be updated to match, along with the date at the top.
