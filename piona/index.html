What Piona is for

Three things Piona is built around.

As many playlists as you like

Create and name your own playlists and put them in any order. Reorder episodes, start from any one of them, and undo a removal. Finished episodes can leave a playlist on their own if you turn that on.

Folders of your own audio

Link a folder of audio files and it shows up in your library as a show, next to the ones you follow. Recordings and lectures get the same player, playlists and progress tracking.

Explicit shows stay hidden

Explicit shows and their episodes stay hidden while the setting is off. Where available, Piona asks your platform for an age-appropriate experience result; access requires adult confirmation and your choice. Piona does not store your age range.

Everyday listening

The rest of the player.

Every episode in one place

The Episodes tab lists every episode from the shows you follow, newest first. Filter it to what you have not started, what you are part-way through, what is in a playlist, or what you have downloaded.

Listen offline

Download episodes for the train, the plane or a weak signal. Piona plays the downloaded file whenever it has one, and can delete downloads once you have finished them.

Pick up where you stopped

Piona remembers your place in every episode, whether it came from a feed or from a folder of your own.

Skip buttons skip 15 seconds

The Lock Screen buttons, and a double or triple press on your headphones, skip 15 seconds forward or back rather than jumping to another episode.

Privacy

Piona's direct content requests.

Piona has no server of its own. These are the content requests it makes directly. Age-appropriate experience checks run through your platform's service.

What leaves your device

What stays on your device

Piona keeps these in its private on-device storage. Deleting the app removes that storage; existing device backups may retain copies under your platform's settings.

  • The shows you follow, your playlists, listening progress and history, and cached feeds
  • Downloaded audio and cached artwork
  • Permission to read any folder you linked
  • Your settings, including each folder's own
